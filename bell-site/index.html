Cheat sheet

  • Tap any corner once: armed (one short buzz). Tap again: disarmed (no buzz).
  • Next bell tap clunks; it stays stuck until opened.
  • Twist the jammed bell: drag your finger around its edge for one full turn to unscrew the dome.
  • Flick the card off the left, right or bottom edge.
  • Two-finger tap anywhere: reset.
  • Triple-tap top-left corner: this screen.
